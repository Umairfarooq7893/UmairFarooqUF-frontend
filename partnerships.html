About Me

I am currently a B.Tech IT student, but my work extends beyond the classroom. I am an explorer, a builder, and someone deeply interested in connecting with interesting people.

Through this platform, I am building a recognizable personal brand and establishing a strong network in the technology and business ecosystem. I am not an established entrepreneur yet, but I am actively working on ideas, research, and collaborations that I plan to develop into something meaningful.

I connect with school principals, CEOs, CTOs, founders, and academicians to discuss meaningful subjects. Whether it's the future of education, advancements in technology, or the realities of building a business, my goal is to create a space for informative, professional conversations that create real value.

My Vision

To build a credible independent media and research platform that bridges the gap between students, educators, and industry leaders, while exploring entrepreneurship and collaborative opportunities.
