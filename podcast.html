Podcast Summary

My podcast is primarily focused on informative and professional conversations with people from different fields and backgrounds. I speak with educational leaders about the education system, and with CEOs, CTOs, and founders about technology, business, and their professional experiences. The central idea is to make every conversation insightful and valuable.
